María M. Marquez-Sosa

PhD student in Signal and Image Processing | Laboratoire CREATIS | MYRIAD team | Université Claude Bernard Lyon I

🏠 From Mérida, VEN 🇻🇪

🌐 Based in Lyon, FR 🇫🇷

I am a PhD student in Signal and Image Processing at Laboratoire CREATIS (UMR 5220), Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1, INSA-Lyon, CNRS, and Inserm. My research explores the fusion of Deep Learning and Computer Vision for 3D image segmentation and registration.

I graduated Summa Cum Laude from the Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence at Pontificia Universidad Javeriana and was honored as Valedictorian in my Biomedical Engineering Bachelor’s. My research interests include image processing, computer graphics, data analysis, and machine learning-based biomedical applications.
